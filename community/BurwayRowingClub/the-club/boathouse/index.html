Boathouse

Three boat bays, a gym, and a function room looking out over the Thames.

The boathouse was extended in 1992 and has three boat-bays, a gym, in-door training facilities and changing rooms (including showers) located downstairs. Upstairs, consists of a bar, a kitchen and a function room overlooking the Thames.
