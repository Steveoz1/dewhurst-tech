Summer regatta season — update

More regattas and more wins for Burway, as the Summer holidays start. At Maidenhead, wins for DJ & Eddie in 2x, Joel, Olly, Evie in singles plus Evie & Amelie in 2x.

Then Bella and Ellie did the long trip to the North of England Sprints and won in their 2x.

What a great summer of regatta competition and success.

Following on from Kingston (and previous) successes, Burway crews were racing again at Molesey and Staines regattas.

At Molesey, there were 2 more wins on the Saturday, for the Seniors, and then the Juniors won 4 more events, on the Sunday.

Heading then to Staines, up river and through Penton Hook lock for all our 18 entries. A logistical triumph for John Biddle who coordinated the whole fleet and made sure everybody was in the right position at the right time, with the right boats and blades, and all came back, safely. As to the racing, a most memorable day with 21 Burway competitors and 8 events won. Fantastic achievement from all parts of the club and we were very well supported by friends and family. Well done all!

It has been a terrific summer of racing for many Burway crews, with wins and medals at Marlow Spring, Ball Cup, Walton & Weybridge, Marlow Town, Reading Town and Kingston, with more to come. Lots of hard work has gone into the performances which have been fantastic. The support from friends and families on the riverbank has been superb and, as always a massive thank you to the coaches and to the trailer drivers, without whom we could not take part in the regattas.

Racing28 March 2026moved from the homepage

Back at the Head of the River Race after 24 years

28.3.2026 - After a gap of 24 years, Burway was back competing at the Head of the River Race. To celebrate 100 years of the HORR, there was a special Mixed Category for the first time. Unfortunately, our boat was rendered unusable, having suffered damage in the morning of the race, and there followed various desperate attempts to borrow a boat to race in. We are extremely grateful to Emanuel School who came up with a boat for our crew. After many hours of waiting, both off and then on the water, the Burway crew raced magnificently, in extremely difficult circumstances, to get to the finish in 4th place in their category, beating nearly 50 other crews in the process. WELL DONE to all the crew and THANK YOU to everyone who has helped them on the journey, including those who have rowed or coxed the VIII in training, all the coaches, and particularly John and Simon for the many hours spent cleaning, sanding and polishing the boat (that then couldn't be used), plus all the fantastic supporters. Great job and we all know what to do next year....
