Directions from Chertsey town centre

Go along Bridge Road towards Chertsey Bridge. Go over the bridge and turn immediately left into Thameside. Follow this road along the side of the river for about a mile, past Laleham Caravan Park and Laleham Park, and you will find the Club on the right hand side. If you get to the bend where Thameside turns sharp right and becomes Ferry Lane, you’ve missed it!

Directions from Staines town centre

Take the Laleham road (B376) out of Staines. In Laleham, you will come to a T-junction with a mini roundabout. Turn right. Follow the road around the sharp left hand bend (Church on your left) and take the second road on your right, Ferry Lane. Follow this road which leads down to the river and then bends left into Thameside. The club is about 200 metres along on the left hand side, just past the public car park.

Directions from Shepperton/Walton

Take B375 right into Thameside. Follow this road along the side of the river for about a mile, past Laleham Caravan Park and Laleham Park, and you will find the Club on the right hand side. If you get to the bend where Thameside turns sharp right and becomes Ferry Lane, you’ve missed it
