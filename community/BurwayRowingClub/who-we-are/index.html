Why we bother writing this down

Most clubs describe themselves in a single paragraph and hope you sort out the rest for yourself. We would rather be specific, because the honest bits are the useful bits.

You will find a section on every page below called “where we'll be straight with you”. Those are the things we cannot do, or will not do. Almost every one of them exists because of a deliberate decision to protect the club as a whole — the tribe of people who turn up every week, in every weather, and hold the place together. We are a small volunteer club. Saying yes to everything would mean doing everything badly, and the people who would feel that first are the members who are here the most.

So we say no to some things on purpose. And we try to say it early, warmly, and to your face — rather than letting you find out in June.

Find yourself

Most people move between two or three of these over a season, and plenty sit in two at once. They are not labels — they are just a way of making sure nobody gets forgotten when we plan the year.

The engine room

The Racing Squad

You want to race properly, and you want a pint afterwards. Both count. In 2026 you became the engine room of this club.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You train three to five times a week and you mean it
  • You want a seat in a good crew and you're prepared to earn it
  • You'd also quite like to know where everyone's going for food afterwards

What you get from Burway

  • A published 8–12 week training block, keyed to the race calendar
  • Two or three coached sessions a week
  • A race calendar out early enough to book time off around
  • A fair, stated route into the faster boats
A typical weekTwo coached water sessions midweek, two at the weekend, and whatever you add yourself.

Where we'll be straight with you

If your availability drops off for a while, you may not hold a seat in a crew that's been training together. We'll tell you that in advance rather than let you find out on a Saturday — and we'll get you back in as soon as you're back.

Never rowed before

Brand New

You've never been in a boat. Good. That's how most of us started.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You saw the club from the towpath and wondered
  • You want to get fit doing something more interesting than a gym
  • You are quietly worried you'll be the worst one there. You won't be, and nobody is counting

What you get from Burway

  • A Learn to Row course with proper structured coaching
  • A clear set of milestones, so you always know what you're working towards
  • A first race chosen because you can do it, not because we need a crew
  • An invitation to the social side from day one, not once you're any good
A typical weekTwo sessions a week to start, in a stable boat, with a coach who is watching only you and two other people.

Where we'll be straight with you

We are a volunteer club, so our beginner coaching runs in blocks rather than continuously. If you join between courses there may be a short wait — and you'll be on the water with us in the meantime.

Chasing something bigger

The Elite-Aspirant

You want to go as far as this sport will take you. We'd love to watch you try.

You're probably one of us if…

  • Ten to twelve sessions a week doesn't sound unreasonable to you
  • You're already thinking about a season, not a race
  • You know most of the work is yours to do

What you get from Burway

  • Water access that fits your volume, and a boat that's right for the job
  • Honest technical feedback from a coach who'll tell you the truth
  • Support with entries into events above our usual circuit
  • A club that will be genuinely delighted for you
A typical weekWhatever your programme says. We'll fit around it and review it with you at every block boundary.

Where we'll be straight with you

We cannot give you a full-time, individually coached daily programme — because the coach who'd do it is the only one the rest of the club has. That's a deliberate choice about who we are, not a lack of ambition for you. If a full-time programme is what you need right now, we'll help you find the right club and we'll be pleased to have had you.

Fitness, fresh air & friendship

Masters

Some of us have been here longer than the rest of the club has been alive. We are not going anywhere.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You come down for the fitness, the fresh air and the company — in whatever order
  • You have been part of this club a long time, and quite possibly your children came through it too
  • You race occasionally in masters categories, or you have been thinking about it

What you get from Burway

  • Masters taken seriously in the weekly plan, not fitted in around everyone else
  • Crews coordinated well enough to actually train together
  • Coaching that is considered rather than an afterthought
  • Training built around recovery, and two or three target regattas a season if you want them
A typical weekTwo or three sessions, technique-led, planned around what your body and your diary will take.

Where we'll be straight with you

Different ages, abilities and availabilities make this genuinely hard to get right, and we will not always manage it. Masters needs sometimes collide with senior and junior needs, and occasionally we all want the same boat on the same morning. When that happens we will say which way we have gone, and why. What we will not do is quietly deprioritise you — a great deal of the governance and stewardship that keeps this club running comes from this group, and the club knows it.

Age 12–18

Junior Racers

The biggest, loudest and probably the happiest part of this club.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You've done a bit and now you want to race
  • Your mates are here, which is honestly most of the reason
  • You want to know when the next regatta is

What you get from Burway

  • A published rolling training block so you and your parents can plan
  • Four sessions a week — two at the weekend, two evenings
  • The same race-week routine every time, so it stops being nerve-wracking
  • A camping weekend, socials, and a squad that feels like a team
A typical weekTuesday and Thursday evenings, Saturday and Sunday mornings.

Where we'll be straight with you

We are careful with our youngest and smallest rowers, and sometimes that means you'll wait a bit longer for a certain boat or a certain event than you'd like. It's about your body, not your ability — and we'll always explain the reason rather than just saying no.

New to the water

Junior Development

First time in a boat, first time on the river, first time in a squad.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You're twelve, thirteen, fourteen, and you've never done this
  • You want to be on the water with other people your age
  • You want to be good enough to get picked

What you get from Burway

  • Patient coaching in small groups with a high coach-to-rower ratio
  • Kit and boats sized for you — not adult kit you have to cope with
  • Clear steps towards the racing squad, so you know where you're going
  • Parents kept properly informed about times, kit and weather
A typical weekTwo structured sessions a week, skills first, with volume kept deliberately low.

Where we'll be straight with you

Being ready in skill isn't the same as being ready in body. Sometimes we'll hold you back from an event or a boat on size or age grounds even though you can do it — that call is about keeping you safe and in this sport for the next twenty years.

Away, but still ours

Remote & Returning

University, work, a new baby, an injury. The door stays open.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You learned to row here and you still call it your club
  • You're away most of the year but you're back in the holidays
  • You want to race in Burway kit when you can

What you get from Burway

  • Our published training plans wherever you are
  • Target events every year designed with you in mind — Burway Head, the club regatta, the camping weekend
  • A named contact who keeps you on the list while you're away
  • A genuinely warm welcome back, every single time
A typical weekWhatever your term allows. Come and row when you're home — you don't need to ask.

Where we'll be straight with you

Seats in crews go to the people training with those crews. If you're back for a fortnight, you're very welcome in a boat — but we won't move eight people who trained all winter. It isn't a rule about you; it's the same rule for everyone, and it's what keeps the club fair for the people here every week.

The people who make it happen

Coaches, Volunteers & Parents

You may never race. You are still the reason this club exists.

You're probably one of us if…

  • You came to watch and somehow ended up on the committee
  • You have a car that can tow, and a weekend you were going to waste anyway
  • You'd help if somebody told you exactly what was needed

What you get from Burway

  • A defined job with a start and an end — not an open-ended ask
  • A route into a coordinating role without having to become a qualified coach
  • Coaching qualifications supported where we can
  • Public, specific thanks. By name.
A typical weekWhatever you offer. Two hours is a real contribution and we'll treat it as one.

Where we'll be straight with you

We've historically leaned on the same handful of people until they were exhausted. We're trying to stop doing that. If we ask you for something and it's more than you can give, say so — that's a completely acceptable answer here.

Not sure which one you are?

That's completely normal, and it doesn't matter. Come down to the club, talk to whoever's on the bank, and we'll work it out together. Nobody at Burway has ever been turned away for not knowing what they wanted yet.

Never rowed?

Watch for our next Learn to Row course or Open Day. No kit, no experience, no fitness requirement.

Rowed before?

Come for a session and we'll find the right squad. Bring whatever kit you've got.

Not going to row at all?

Also fine. Some of the most important people in this club have never been in a boat.
