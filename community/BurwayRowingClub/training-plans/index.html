One published block, two squads. Every session is written in three layers: what the development crews do, what the racers add on top, and a stretch target for anyone chasing something bigger. Same session, same water, same time — more of it.

Roughly 12–13  →  Development crews Roughly 14–15  →  Racers 16 and over  →  Stretch target

Those brackets are a guide, not a rule. Pick the layer that matches where you are, not your birthday — and move up when you are ready. For juniors, midweek is land: Tuesday and Thursday are ergo and circuits at the club, and the water is for weekends. The stretch group adds a fifth session on Wednesday, a home circuit you do in your own time.

For the senior squad. Four sessions a week is the expectation, two of them coached. The elite-aspirant programme sits on top of this as the stretch layer, not beside it.

Land training
Land
Development crews20 minutes at rate 20 on the ergo, then stretch. Log it.

FocusThe same session every week for fifteen weeks. That is the point of it - it is the one you measure everything else against. Stretch before and after. Hamstrings especially. 10-15 minutes to be effective.
