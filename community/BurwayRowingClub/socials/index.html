Between now and Christmas

You do not need to row to come to any of these. Partners, parents, children, friends and people who have never been in a boat are all genuinely welcome — that is not a polite formula, it is how these evenings actually work.

13Sep
Barbecue

Autumn Club Social

The whole club, one afternoon at the boathouse. Barbecue, the summer's results and photos, junior presentations, and the first proper look at the autumn season. Bring family, bring food, bring the people who have never seen where you disappear to at six in the morning.

WhenSunday 13 September
Timefrom 1:00pm
WhereThe boathouse
CostFree — bring a dish
Everyone welcome — members, families & guests
16Oct
Quiz

Burway Pub Quiz

The annual test of whether rowers know anything at all. Teams of six, a picture round nobody will get, and a raffle in aid of the boat fund. Turn up on your own and we will find you a team — that is usually how the winning team gets assembled.

WhenFriday 16 October
Time7:30pm start
WhereClubhouse bar
Cost£5 a head
Sign up on the board or just turn up
6Nov
Bonfire

Fireworks Night

Fireworks over the water, a bonfire on the bank, hot dogs, and mulled cider for anyone not driving the trailer in the morning. One of the best views of a firework display anywhere in the borough, and we have it to ourselves.

WhenFriday 6 November
Timegates 6:00pm
WhereThe boathouse & bank
Cost£5 adults, under-16s free
Family event — bring everyone
12Dec
Dinner

Christmas Party

Dinner, drinks, the club awards, and the annual reminder that rowers cannot dance. Black tie optional and enthusiastically ignored by most. Tickets go on sale in November and it does sell out, so do not leave it.

WhenSaturday 12 December
Time7:00pm
WhereTBC — venue being booked
CostTBC
Tickets from November · members & guests

Want to help run one?

It is a small job

Each of these needs one person to own it and two or three to help on the night. That is genuinely all. If you have ever thought “I would help but I do not know what with” — this is what with.

You do not have to row

Some of the best social organisers this club has ever had have never been in a boat. Parents, partners and retired members are all very welcome to take one on.

Bring an idea

Four events is a starting point, not a limit. If you want to run something else — a curry night, a river clean, a quiz on the water — say so and we will back it.
