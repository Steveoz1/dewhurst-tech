Welcome to the Burway Head page.

The 2027 Burway Head will take place on Saturday 20th February 2027

 

The 2026 Burway Head is on Saturday 21st February and entries are now open on BROE2.

On a delightful stretch of the river, it is an upstream course of about 3,000 metres, starting from just above the M3 bridge near Chertsey Lock, and finishing just downstream of Penton Hook Lock. It is on the Thames, so there are bends, which make it even more fun (.......and challenging).

Events are available for 8+. 8x+, 4+, 4-, 4x+, 4x-, with prizes for all winners where 3 or more crews compete in an event.

There will be 2 divisions, at 10.30 am and 1pm. - The fee is £18 per seat (excl coxes). All fees must be paid by 14th Feb. 

Please note that in the event of cancellation we will make a full refund of all entry fees paid.

Coaches must ensure that all instructions, marshalling, safety plan and course details etc, are read and understood by your crews. If you have any questions, please click here.

We look forward to seeing you on 21st February.
