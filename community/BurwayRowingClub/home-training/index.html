Four ways to run the club circuit at home. The Wednesday session on the junior stretch plan is one of these — the plan says which. Nothing here needs a gym, a weight or more than a chair and a flight of stairs.

Every circuit 1 minute per exercise · 30 seconds rest · 2 to 3 sets · 10 minute warm-up first

Pick a circuit

The full circuit

Circuit A

The coaches' sheet exactly as written. The default, and the one to use if in doubt.

The circuit

Squats, plank, side plank (both sides), press ups, tricep dips, step ups, V sits or sit and twist, stair climbers, front lunge, back lunge, aleknas, glute bridge.

How to run it

1 minute per exercise, 30 seconds rest. 2 to 3 sets, continuous, as time permits. 10 minute whole-body warm up before starting. Substitution is allowed, keeping the same reps. Make sure every exercise is carried out correctly - NO SHORT CUTS.

Legs and core

Circuit B

Legs and trunk. Good in a week where the water work has been all technique and not much pressure.

The circuit

Squats, front lunge, back lunge, step ups, stair climbers, squat jumps, glute bridge, plank, side plank, V sits, aleknas.

How to run it

1 minute per exercise, 30 seconds rest. 2 to 3 sets, continuous, as time permits. 10 minute whole-body warm up before starting. Substitution is allowed, keeping the same reps. Make sure every exercise is carried out correctly - NO SHORT CUTS.

Upper body and core

Circuit C

Arms, shoulders and trunk. Good in a week with heavy leg work on the water.

The circuit

Press ups, tricep dips, plank, side plank, V sits or sit and twist, aleknas, crunches, dorsals, squat thrusts, glute bridge.

How to run it

1 minute per exercise, 30 seconds rest. 2 to 3 sets, continuous, as time permits. 10 minute whole-body warm up before starting. Substitution is allowed, keeping the same reps. Make sure every exercise is carried out correctly - NO SHORT CUTS.

The short one

Circuit D

Twenty minutes rather than forty. For a race week, or a week when there genuinely is not time.

The circuit

Squats, press ups, plank, step ups, glute bridge, aleknas. One set only, straight through.

How to run it

1 minute per exercise, 30 seconds rest. 2 to 3 sets, continuous, as time permits. 10 minute whole-body warm up before starting. Substitution is allowed, keeping the same reps. Make sure every exercise is carried out correctly - NO SHORT CUTS.
