About the founder

Twenty-five years on every side of the table

Dewhurst.tech is led by its founder, Steve Smith. In his own words:

I started as an aircraft engineer building and certifying avionic systems - designing and physically installing the equipment and wiring, modifying the airframe and structures that carried it - and then had to prove, against an airworthiness standard and with my name on it, that the aircraft was safe to fly. A signature on a certificate is not the same thing as a safe aeroplane. Fit for service is more than simply being fit for purpose - and someone has to be able to tell the difference.

I have been doing the same job ever since, across industries where operational safety and quality assurance are critical: oil and gas, chemical, nuclear, marine and aviation. I came through the ranks as a control and automation engineer and met the same problem from every side - engineering and operations, engineering sales and projects, business development, consultancy on the operator's side, then quality assurance, standards, safety and reliability. Most recently I led robotics, sensing, integrity and inspection - and OT strategy - at BP.

The problem I keep meeting is the same one. The technology is rarely the difficulty. The organisation it lands in - the people, the process, the OT infrastructure beneath it and the authority to actually adopt it - is where the value is won or lost.

Today I do that work independently, through Dewhurst.tech - as an advisor, a board member or a hands-on lead, depending on what the decision needs.
