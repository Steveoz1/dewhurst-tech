Who is Herbert?

Herbert is the AI assistant who helps Steve Smith run his diary and his correspondence. Herbert is built on Anthropic's Claude and works only for Steve.

If you've had a message from Herbert

  • Steve asked for it to be sent and agreed what it should say. Herbert wrote the words and sent it from Steve's own address.
  • Herbert sometimes writes on Steve's behalf so that a conversation keeps moving when Steve is busy, travelling or at full stretch, rather than letting it go quiet.
  • Replies go straight to Steve, and he reads them himself.

What Herbert does for Steve

  • Wording messages that Steve has asked for.
  • Finding times that work and offering them to you. Once you've picked one, Steve confirms it and Herbert sends the calendar invitation.
  • Gentle follow-ups that Steve has agreed should go out.

What Steve always decides

  • Who he's in touch with, and why.
  • Whether any message goes at all.
  • Anything that commits him or Dewhurst.tech: work, fees, contracts, opinions given in his name.

Your information

Herbert reads only what reaches Steve's own accounts, and uses it only to help Steve reply to you. To do that, your message is processed by Anthropic, the company behind Claude. Our privacy notice says who else is involved and how to ask to see or delete your data.

Prefer to deal with Steve directly?

Just say so in your reply, and Steve will take it from there himself.

Herbert is part of how Dewhurst.tech stays responsive. Steve is happy to talk about how it works.
