Privacy notice

How Dewhurst.tech handles personal data

Last updated 19 October 2026.

The short version. Reading this site is not used to identify you. The Value & ROI Calculator runs in your browser; when you save, open or print a case, an anonymised copy of its figures is kept by Dewhurst.tech, as set out in the calculator data policy. The email that brings each copy also shows your device's internet address. Your contact details are only sent if you ask us to contact you. Nothing is ever sold.

Who is responsible

Dewhurst.tech Ltd, registered in England & Wales, company number 17373526, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. For anything in this notice, including a request to see or delete your data, email steve@dewhurst.tech.

What is collected, and when

1. Reading the website

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies and does not track individuals across sites. It processes some technical information about each visit - the page viewed, roughly when, the country, the referring site and the browser type - but it does not keep anything that identifies you.

2. Emailing directly

If you email steve@dewhurst.tech, that correspondence is held in a Microsoft 365 mailbox for as long as it is useful, in the ordinary way that business correspondence is.

3. The Value & ROI Calculator

The calculator runs inside your own browser. While you work, nothing is sent. When you save, open or print a case - which needs your agreement in step 7 - an anonymised copy of its figures is sent to Dewhurst.tech. It contains no names, no organisation and none of the descriptions you typed. The form service that carries it adds your device's internet address to the email. We don't use that address to work out who you are. The full detail is in the calculator data policy.

Your name and organisation are optional. They appear on a report you print, and are sent to Dewhurst.tech only if you tick the box asking to be contacted. Saved files and reports go to your own device.

If you tick the box asking us to contact you about a case, your name, organisation, email (and phone, if given) and the case's name and description are sent with the copy. We use them only to talk that case through with you, on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. The figures may be commercially sensitive to your organisation: send only what you are comfortable sharing.

What is never done

  • Your data is never sold, and never passed to a third party for their marketing.
  • You will not be added to a mailing list, newsletter or automated follow-up sequence.
  • No single case is ever published, quoted or described, even anonymously; only combined trends from many cases.
  • There is no customer account or login.

Who else is involved

  • Microsoft 365 - hosts the mailbox, where the anonymised calculator copies arrive and are kept (see the calculator data policy).
  • Web3Forms - passes each calculator copy on to that mailbox by email, and adds the sender's internet address. Its terms allow it to keep submissions for up to three years, and it may pass the internet address to a spam-checking service.
  • GitHub Pages - serves the website itself. Like any web host, it logs visitors' IP addresses for security and operation. It never sees calculator data.
  • GoatCounter - provides the analytics described above.

Data may be processed outside the UK by these suppliers, under the safeguards their standard terms provide.

How long it is kept

Email is kept for as long as it is useful to the conversation it belongs to - and reviewed at least once a year. Where there is no live conversation, it is deleted after two years. The same applies to contact details sent from the calculator. Anonymised calculator copies hold no personal data apart from the internet address on the email that brings them; how long they are kept is in the calculator data policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its being held. Email steve@dewhurst.tech and it will be dealt with within one month, normally far sooner. There is no charge and no form to fill in. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner.

Changes
